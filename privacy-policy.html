CarX

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · App: com.markjosephbolivar.carx · Developer: Mark Joseph Bolivar

In short: CarX sends the photo and prompt you choose to Google's Gemini model through Firebase so it can create a preview. Previews stay on your device. We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve the app, and we never sell personal information.

1. Summary

  • CarX does not require an account and does not run its own photo storage servers.
  • The photo you select and the modification you describe are sent to Google's Gemini image model through Firebase AI Logic to generate a preview.
  • Generated previews are stored only on your device until you delete them.
  • We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage and fix crashes. We do not sell personal information.
  • Every result is labeled AI-generated, and you can report unsafe output from the result screen.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Photos and prompts

When you start a build, CarX reads the photo you selected and sends the image bytes plus your text prompt (or the selected style and detail options) to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic. The request is authorized with an anonymous Firebase Authentication session and protected by Firebase App Check.

CarX does not upload your photos to servers operated by the developer and does not keep a copy in any developer-operated database. Temporary processing happens inside Google's Firebase infrastructure as needed to return the result. Google may retain requests for a limited period for abuse monitoring and safety under its terms. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

Please avoid uploading photos that show people, license plates, home addresses, or other private information unless you have the right to do so.

2.2 Information stored on your device

CarX stores generated previews and thumbnails in the app's documents directory, a reference to the original photo, your prompt and selected modifications, and app preferences (theme, haptics, reduced motion, onboarding completion, and AI disclosure acceptance). You can delete builds in the Garage, clear all builds and cache in Settings, or uninstall the app.

2.4 Reports you submit

If you report an AI result, CarX records the reason, result identifier, generation mode, and a diagnostic event. This is used to review safety issues and improve the service. Do not include personal information in a report.

2.5 Information we do not collect

CarX does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, health data, or payment information. CarX does not currently offer purchases or subscriptions.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / camera: used only when you choose a photo. On modern Android versions the system photo picker limits access to the item you select.
  • Internet: required to reach Firebase services.

4. How we use information

We use the information to generate and save the preview you request, keep the service secure, diagnose crashes, understand feature usage, respond to safety reports, and comply with the law. We do not sell personal information, do not use your photos to train our own models, and do not use your information for third-party advertising.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract (generating your preview), legitimate interests (security, diagnostics, improvement), consent where required (which you can withdraw), and legal obligation where applicable.

6. Retention

  • Local builds remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data is retained according to the retention setting configured in Firebase (2 or 14 months, depending on the project setting).
  • Crashlytics data is retained for up to 90 days by default.
  • Report records are kept only as long as needed to investigate and prevent recurrence.

7. Sharing

We share data only with Google Firebase as our service provider, with authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety, and with a successor entity if the app is transferred. We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete builds in the Garage or clear all local data in Settings.
  • Deny or revoke photo access in Android system settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of data linked to your app instance.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because CarX uses an anonymous identifier, we may need the app instance ID or approximate event time to locate data. We respond within the time required by applicable law.

9. Children

CarX is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can delete it.

10. Security

We use encrypted transport, Firebase App Check with Play Integrity, scoped service credentials, and platform sandboxing. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. AI-generated content

Previews are synthetic images created by an AI model. They can be inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected, and they are labeled as AI-generated in the app. Do not rely on a preview as an engineering plan, a safety assessment, or a representation of a real vehicle. You can report a result from the result screen.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date here and in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
